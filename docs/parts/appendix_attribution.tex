\chapter{Image Credits}\label{C:Attribution}

	The following images are used in this document. We are grateful to their creators, and to the organisations which make them freely available.

	\begin{description}
		\item[Typewriter] Used on the cover and on the title page of the Appendices.

		      \textit{Keyboard, Typewriter, Antiquity} by \texttt{bjoern-hiekel}, from \forcelink{https://pixabay.com/photos/keyboard-typewriter-antiquity-old-7598900/}{Pixabay}. Used under the Pixabay Content License.

		\item[The Writing Desk] Used on the title page of Part I.

		      Childe Hassam, \textit{The Writing Desk} (1915), etching in black on wove paper. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Corcoran Collection (Bequest of Frank B. Bristow, 1968), accession no.~2015.19.1530. Available through the National Gallery of Art's \forcelink{https://www.nga.gov/artworks/169560-writing-desk}{Open Access} programme, under a CC0 licence.

		\item[Costume] Used on the title page of Part II.

		      Mary E. Humes, \textit{Costume} (c.~1941), watercolour, graphite, and pen and ink on paper. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Index of American Design, accession no.~1943.8.9509. Available through the National Gallery of Art's \forcelink{https://www.nga.gov/artworks/21605-costume}{Open Access} programme, under a CC0 licence.

		\item[Desk] Used on the title page of Part III.

		      Ferdinand Cartier, \textit{Desk} (c.~1940), graphite on paperboard. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Index of American Design, accession no.~1943.8.5338. Available through the National Gallery of Art's \forcelink{https://www.nga.gov/artworks/17539-desk}{Open Access} programme, under a CC0 licence.
		\item[Enrag'd Monster] Used on the example cover pages in the theme chapters.

		      John Hamilton Mortimer, \textit{Enrag'd Monster} (1778), etching. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Ailsa Mellon Bruce Fund, accession no.~1974.53.22. Available through the National Gallery of Art's \forcelink{https://www.nga.gov/artworks/54438-enragd-monster}{Open Access} programme, under a CC0 licence.

		\item[The Mermaid] Used on the example part pages in the theme chapters.

		      Frederick Stuart Church, \textit{The Mermaid} (c.~1881), etching in green ink. National Gallery of Art, Washington, Reba and Dave Williams Collection, Gift of Reba and Dave Williams, accession no.~2008.115.1238. Available through the National Gallery of Art's \forcelink{https://www.nga.gov/artworks/148040-mermaid}{Open Access} programme, under a CC0 licence.

		\item[Paper Texture] Used for the pages of the chapter on the \texttt{dnd} theme, and available to documents through \cmdref{RpgSetDndPaper}.

		      From \forcelink{https://lostandtaken.com/}{Lost and Taken}, by way of the \forcelink{https://github.com/rpgtex/DND-5e-LaTeX-Template}{D\&D 5e \LaTeX{} Template}, from which \rpgtex{} is forked. Lost and Taken's terms allowed its textures to be used royalty-free, with no attribution required.

		\item[The Crown Jewels] Used for the example items in the chapters on the default and \texttt{dnd} themes.

		      Blaise Alexandre Desgoffe, \textit{Les Joyaux de la Couronne} (\textit{The Crown Jewels}; 19th century), painting. From \forcelink{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The-crown-jewels-blaise-alexandre-desgoffe.jpg}{Wikimedia Commons}, where it is in the public domain, as a faithful reproduction of a public domain work of art.

		\item[Phaser Model] Used for the example item in the chapter on the \texttt{scifi} theme.

		      Marcin Wichary, \textit{star trek phaser model} (2008), photograph, from \forcelink{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phaser_model.jpg}{Wikimedia Commons} (originally published on Flickr). Used under the \forcelink{https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/}{CC BY 2.0} licence; the image has been reduced in size.
